Integration bundle built against the uploaded GHOST ui.py and main.py.
Before applying, back up your current ui.py and main.py.
Copy the bundled ui.py and main.py over the matching files in your GHOST root.
Copy actions/akkhor/ and config/akkhor_config.json into the same relative paths.
The panel implements login plus read-only GET requests for configured resources.
The endpoint paths are configurable because exact Swagger response schemas were not supplied.
No database connection is made; all calls go through Akkhor's HTTP API.
